Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2024

Welcome to AADHYA Telecom Infrastructure Services. Your privacy and the security of your technical data are paramount to our operations. This Privacy Policy details our protocols for handling information collected during our telecommunication maintenance services.

01. Information Collection

We systematically collect data essential for operational integrity and service optimization. This includes:

  • check_circle Operational Data: Tower diagnostics, maintenance logs, and performance metrics.
  • check_circle Personnel Information: Contact details and credentials of authorized personnel interacting with our portals.
  • check_circle Telemetry Data: Automated reports generated by remote monitoring equipment.

02. Use of Information

The data we gather is strictly utilized to maintain and enhance infrastructure reliability. Specific applications include:

  • check_circle Executing scheduled and emergency maintenance protocols.
  • check_circle Analyzing system performance to predict and prevent hardware failures.
  • check_circle Ensuring compliance with regional telecommunications safety standards.

03. Data Protection

AADHYA employs industrial-grade cybersecurity measures to protect all technical and personal data. We utilize end-to-end encryption for telemetry transmission and maintain secure, access-controlled databases for all operational records. Regular security audits are conducted to ensure system resilience against unauthorized access.

04. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent data to external entities. Information is only shared under the following conditions:

  • check_circle With certified subcontractors actively engaged in specific maintenance tasks (under strict NDA).
  • check_circle When mandated by regulatory authorities or law enforcement agencies.

05. User Rights

Authorized clients retain full rights over their proprietary infrastructure data. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of specific operational records by contacting our compliance division at compliance@aadhyatelecom.com. Requests are processed within standard SLA timeframes.
